# Graduation is a rename

Every sandbox is a standalone repo from the moment it exists. Work
that proves worthwhile graduates by being renamed, usually to drop the
dotted prefix (`prototype.foo` becomes `foo`), though the new name may
vary. Nothing is copied out and nothing is extracted; the repo and its
history are already their own.

What graduation changes in the parent is its binding to that repo,
and only that.
