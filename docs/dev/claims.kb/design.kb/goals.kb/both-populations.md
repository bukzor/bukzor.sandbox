# New sandboxes are born here; existing ones may migrate in

New sandboxes are created under the parent. Existing prefixed
siblings (`prototype.*`, `research.*`, `ideation.*`, `scratch.*`) may
be bound to the parent as well, one at a time, as it is convenient.
Neither population waits on the other.
